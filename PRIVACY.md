# Privacy Policy — DAS Image Converter

**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026

**DAS Image Converter** is a 100% offline Windows desktop application. Your privacy is guaranteed by design.

## 1. Data Collection
- **No personal data is collected.** We do not require accounts, logins, names, email addresses, or identifiers.
- **No analytics, telemetry, or advertising.** The app includes zero tracking SDKs, zero crash-reporting uploads, and zero ads.
- **No network access.** The app does not declare internet client capabilities and never connects to remote servers.

## 2. Image & File Handling
- All images you select or drag into DAS Image Converter are decoded, resized, converted, and exported **exclusively on your local device**.
- Your images never leave your computer and are never transmitted to or stored on any server.
- Temporary conversion files created inside your local app temp directory are removed automatically when you clear results, start a new batch, or close the application.
- The app only accesses files you explicitly choose via the Windows file picker or drag-and-drop, and only writes exported images or ZIP archives to destinations you explicitly select.

## 3. Local Settings
- Your theme preference (`System`, `Light`, or `Dark`) is stored locally on your device via standard Windows application settings (`ApplicationData.LocalSettings`). It is never transmitted elsewhere.
